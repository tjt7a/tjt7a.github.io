AutoFFinder is a reconfigurable hardware-accelerated platform for multi-guide CRISPR/Cas9 off-target search, published in *Bioinformatics* and presented at RECOMBArch 2026.

**Software:** ReLev, a runtime-reconfigurable FPGA engine for CRISPR binding site discovery, is under development as open source.
