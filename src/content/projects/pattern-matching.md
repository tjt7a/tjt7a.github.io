RapidScan, a parameterized HLS streaming string-matching library for FPGAs, took 3rd place in the 2026 FCCM Reconfigurable Computing Challenge. Related work covers normal forms for Perl-compatible regular expressions, synthesizing FPGA designs from legacy string-processing code, and debugging support for pattern-matching languages and accelerators.
