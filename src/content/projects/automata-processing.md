This line of work, including my dissertation (*Accelerating Decision Tree Ensemble Inference with an Automata Representation*, 2019), showed how to map machine learning models such as decision tree ensembles onto automata-processing hardware. Part of it was done during research internships at Micron in 2015 and 2016. It also produced benchmark suites that are widely used in automata architecture research.

**Software:** AutomataZoo, an automata benchmark suite, and RFAutomata, a tool that converts decision trees to automata.
